Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the WikiMe app for Android (package uk.jj.wikime) and this website. WikiMe is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • WikiMe only talks to Wikipedia's own servers. It cannot reach any other site.
  • The developer runs no servers and receives nothing from the app.
  • What you save, read and highlight stays on your phone, encrypted.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reports.

What Wikipedia sees

To show you an article, WikiMe asks Wikipedia for it, as any reader does. Wikipedia is run by the Wikimedia Foundation, and its privacy policy covers those requests. WikiMe sends no cookies and no account, and identifies itself only as WikiMe, as Wikimedia asks apps to do. With the Tor switch on, Wikipedia sees a Tor address instead of yours.

Address lookups

Before connecting, a phone looks up Wikipedia's address. WikiMe does this over an encrypted connection to Quad9 (or Cloudflare if Quad9 cannot be reached), so your network cannot see which Wikipedia pages are looked up. You can switch this off in Settings if a network blocks it. With Tor on, no lookup is made on the phone at all.

What stays on your phone

Saved articles and their pictures, reading lists, history, recent searches, highlights and notes are kept in the app's private storage, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held in Android's Keystore. Even the file names are scrambled. Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for WikiMe.

Widgets

The home screen widgets show only Wikipedia's public front page: the featured article and on this day. They are refreshed when you open Explore, and fetch for themselves only while Tor is off, so a widget can never send anything outside Tor.

Links to this website

Settings has Check for a newer version and More apps. Each opens this site in your browser; WikiMe itself never contacts it.

Keeping and deleting your data

History can be kept for a day, a week, 30 days, 90 days or always, cleared at any time, or not recorded at all in private reading. Saved articles and highlights are removed in the app, and deleted files are overwritten first. Uninstalling WikiMe deletes everything it holds.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the app list. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
