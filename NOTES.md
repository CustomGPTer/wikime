WikiMe 1.0.0: a private reader for Wikipedia on Android 10 and later.

- Reads Wikipedia and nothing else, and never shows a web address. Every article is rebuilt as a calm page with a large photo, book-style type, a facts box card and folding sections.
- Explore: featured article, picture of the day, in the news, on this day, most read and did you know.
- Optional Tor switch (off by default), encrypted address lookups and a connection pinned to Wikipedia's certificates.
- Saved articles with their pictures for reading offline, reading lists, history with private reading, and highlights with notes, all encrypted on the phone.
- Five themes, text size, serif or sans, spacing, data saver and text only.
- 44 languages, read aloud, Nearby, Look up in WikiMe from other apps, shake for a random article and three home screen widgets.
- App lock with a PIN or fingerprint, screenshot blocking, hide from recent apps and a tablet layout.

No account, no adverts, no tracking.
