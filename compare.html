What WikiMe does not do

WikiMe is for reading only: it cannot edit Wikipedia and has no account, so saved articles do not sync between devices. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself. Tor makes pages slower to load.

Where the others do more

The Wikipedia app syncs reading lists across devices, lets you edit, and installs and updates from Google Play. Kiwix keeps whole downloaded copies of Wikipedia and other sites for reading with no connection at all.
